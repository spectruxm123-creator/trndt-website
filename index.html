About Trendt

TRNDT is a Solana-based token designed for high-value and high-volume payments. It supports transactions for real-world assets — including real estate, vehicles, luxury goods, travel bookings, and construction projects.

Built on the fastest, lowest-cost blockchain, TRNDT enables fast, secure, and borderless transactions for large-scale payments — while maintaining deep liquidity pools for everyday trading.
